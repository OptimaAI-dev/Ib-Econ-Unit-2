# IB Economics HL — Unit 2 Microeconomics Field Guide

A one-page, interactive study guide for the Unit 2 (Microeconomics) test: every definition from
the class notes, animated demand-and-supply diagrams, a shifter lab, the nine P2 questions with a
diagram builder that marks your answer, and Explain drills with mark schemes.

**▶ Open it: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ib-econ-unit2/**

Nothing to install. Works on a phone. If your device is set to reduce motion, the animations turn
themselves off.

---

## What's in it

- **Cram path** — what to revise first if you are short on time, with a countdown to the test.
- **2.1 Demand** — demand, the law of demand, movement vs shift, every non-price determinant, and
  the HL income and substitution effects.
- **2.2 Supply** — supply, the law of supply, shifters, and diminishing marginal returns
  (total, average and marginal product, and why marginal cost rises).
- **2.3 Equilibrium** — the price mechanism, surplus and shortage, and the full chain to a new
  equilibrium after a shift. This is the 10-mark question.
- **Shifter lab** — pick any demand or supply shifter and watch the curves move; it writes the
  chain of reasoning for you.
- **2.4 Behaviour** — biases, choice architecture, and alternative business objectives.
- **Practice papers** — nine P2 demand-and-supply questions where you build the diagram and it
  tells you what's wrong, Explain drills broken into marks, per-section questions, and a 10-mark tab.
- **Self-test and glossary** — quick recall questions and every term in one list.
- **Notes audit** — which lines of the class notes were blank, cut off or wrong, and what the guide
  says instead. Worth reading before you trust your own notes.

## Flashcards

79 term-and-definition cards, same content as the guide:

- Quizlet set: https://quizlet.com/br/1209716109/ib-econ-hl-unit-2-microeconomics-assumptions-flash-cards/
- Or make your own copy: in Quizlet, **Create → Import**, paste `unit2-cards.txt`, and set
  *Between term and definition* to **Tab** and *Between cards* to **New line**.

## Changing it

Everything lives in `index.html`: the styles at the top, the sections in the middle, and the
diagrams and practice questions as JavaScript at the bottom. Edit it, commit, and GitHub Pages
republishes in about a minute.

## Notes

Built from the class Economics Notes (Unit 2 Microeconomics and Assumptions tabs) and the
P2 D&S Questions doc. The pizzeria, bubble tea, beef and bakery numbers are made up to make the
patterns easy to see — the economics is what matters. Use it, fork it, send it on.
